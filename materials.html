AgroNinja · Measuring traffic from trade PR

I worked on pre-launch acquisition, trade PR and international reseller outreach for the cattle-weighing app. We tagged the links in our PR outreach with UTM parameters so we could identify visits from trade coverage in Google Analytics alongside traffic from paid campaigns.

Actual result. We made contact with 150+ resellers internationally and recorded 3,000+ pre-sales registrations.
