What it answers
A controlled opening test needs about 3,400 reached people per version per market (25% baseline, 3-point lift, 5% significance, 80% power, 1.2 impressions per person, highest CPM in the selection). Click channels need about 300 clicks and YouTube about 2,000 views at the low end of the range before versions are compared.

Ranges use each market’s own CPM where one exists and split the budget evenly across the selected markets. Search and YouTube use the same cost range in every market. None of this is Invity data.

Country CPM / Published reference
AdSpendSpy, 2026 estimates, by country. Standard Meta (Facebook and Instagram) campaign scenario. CPM is EUR per 1,000 impressions. A modelled industry reference.
Meta CTR, CPM and CPC / Published reference
adtribute, September 2026, European direct-to-consumer e-commerce, Facebook and Instagram. Mixed campaign objectives in a customer panel. Link CTR 1.19% (middle half 0.92%–1.48%), CPM €12.76 (€9.19–€16.56) and CPC €1.06 (€0.82–€1.28) per link click. The model’s implied CPC uses country CPM and European CTR together.
Finance app CPI / Published reference
Adjust Finance App Insights, published 29 October 2025, data January 2023–August 2025. Mobile user acquisition across paid networks. CPI is spend per attributed install, shown in original USD. Global finance $1.51 (2024) → $1.13 (H1 2025). Global crypto $5.17 → $2.90. In H1 2025, France $5.37 and Germany $7.71. European finance app CTR 1%. Context only. The forecast runs in EUR and uses no USD conversion.

See the first-month test sequence
  • Weeks 1–2: talk to support, product and the people who know each market; read the comments in every language we can.
  • Build a multi-market forecast we can measure against — with CPM, CTR and language versions in one view — and replace the industry defaults with Invity’s account data as soon as it is available.
  • Agree what we want to learn from one useful comparison, and set up measurement so the same test can later be read market by market.
  • Make and review that comparison in the first market where the forecast shows enough volume, with a native-language check and a compliance check. Then decide where to adapt it.
  • End of month: share what we learned, what the sample can and cannot tell us, and which markets are ready for a language version next.
