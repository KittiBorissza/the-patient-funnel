The plan behind it

Plenty of people in Czechia are curious about Bitcoin, yet the first purchase can feel like an exam they haven’t studied for. I want Invity’s social channels to make that step understandable, so people can decide calmly, yes or no.

Independent application proposal. Not an official Invity page. Every number below is a synthetic example, not Invity data.

How it works. Cost per install rises as a channel spends more: base × (1 + 0.15 × spend ÷ €1,000) ÷ scenario factor. First buyers = installs × buy rate, where the scenario factor (Cautious 0.7, Base 1, Upside 1.3) also scales the buy rate, capped at 100%. Referrals are capped at 150 installs a month, because only so many existing customers will invite someone; referral money beyond that is reported as unused. Media and rewards only: no salaries or production. A low spend per first buyer doesn’t prove profit or that the buyers wouldn’t have come anyway.

What I’d test

Seven creatives, each with one question to answer

Each has a hypothesis and one metric. Anything that shows the app or mentions fees, custody or risk goes through Invity’s review first.

A real first purchase, every screen

Reel and Short, 35 seconds, plus a carousel

Idea
Film one real first purchase, from download to just after “confirm”.
Hook
“First time buying bitcoin. Here’s every screen, even the boring ones.”
Call to action
See every step in the Invity app
Hypothesis
Showing the fee and custody screens makes people more likely to start sign-up than a general product ad.
Metric
Share of viewers who watch past the fee screen
