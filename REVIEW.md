## Publication review

- The withdrawal artwork retains the original “Any time” line. Check availability, fees and limits with product before publishing that creative.
- The controlled comparison holds the footage and first frame constant. If production changes the opening visual, call it a comparison of two creative treatments.
- The controlled-test estimate uses independent reached people and an approximate two-proportion calculation. Confirm randomisation, observed reach and event matching before launch. The model uses one test ad set in the primary market when the budget is split.
