The €100 test method · quotes pending
  1. Choose one eligible country, one purchase type, €100 and the same payment method. Use a separate run for a recurring purchase.
  2. Capture each in-app quote in a matched time window. Record payment method, timestamp, fiat paid, provider fee, exchange rate and quoted sats.
  3. Record actual sats received, time to receipt and any send fee. For direct-to-wallet routes, record settlement without a second transfer.
  4. Compare received sats and document any delay or condition that affects comparability.
