## 1. Central narrative and audiences

**Make Bitcoin feel within reach.** I’m Kitti. Here’s how I’d turn Bitcoin questions into social content, first purchases and long-term conversations.

The pitch starts with work people can see. The first Invity purchase is the demonstration campaign. It can serve someone who knows Bitcoin and is evaluating Invity, as well as a curious newcomer. Alongside it, show Bitcoin-native culture and an existing customer’s Auto Buy and custody questions. The full proposal spans a 100-day programme.

Label evidence “Public source”, “Working hypothesis” or “To confirm with Invity”. Label the €100 comparison “Method drafted · quotes pending”. Three audience states remain visible: Bitcoin-aware non-customers, Bitcoin-curious newcomers, and existing buyers. Choose the first test market only after checking existing customer cohorts, language and creative capacity, eligibility and actual campaign data. Czechia is an example candidate, not a decision or assumed account finding.

**Story sequence** Creative → audience question → funnel and platform preview → honest budget and sample decision → 100-day research and execution → proof and CV.

## 7. Budget and experiments

An impression-based watch rate and an independently editable reached-person baseline have different denominators. Show both along with frequency. Four editable monthly Meta presets €500, €1,500, €5,000 and €15,000, clearly example scenarios. One market and one acquisition setup by default. Inputs labelled published reference, illustrative assumption or user-entered. Meta CPM/CPC, finance-app CPI and first-purchase CPA remain separate. Keep references, dates, currencies, sources and conversion explicit.

Primary output: expected event volume in the chosen window, what it can inform and the simplest setup. Direct CPA mode and optional CPM → CTR → install → purchase funnel mode. Market, remarketing and ad-set splits appear in “Adjust setup”, with volume per group and reason for any split.

“Budget for an event target” uses target × cost/event. “Sample for a controlled test” uses baseline, minimum detectable effect, allocation, alpha and power, and compares the required sample to the estimated eligible reach and time. Do not equate purchase count with reach or imply significance from low volume. Forecast paid acquisition volume and controlled reach are different units. Attribution describes a recorded path and does not prove incrementality. Show available and required sample, budget and time together. A first hook test holds footage, opening visual, framing, audience and destination constant; changing the visual too tests whole treatments.

The five-channel market model and nine-market view live behind “Explore the full market plan”. The Meta test is included inside the broader Meta allocation when comparing the same scenario. Never add the same spend twice. Preserve original acquisition planner, full funnel, experiment power, creator pilot, payback and content-time calculators as functional archived tools with inputs, formulas, assumptions and saved query states.

Existing experiment backlog: walkthrough vs feature, local creator, paid amplification, CRM holdout. They are proposed tests, with feasibility and approvals checked before scheduling.

## 9. Claims, risks and unresolved decisions

- Fee, custody, Auto Send, account availability, platform ad eligibility, app screens, translations and Turbo copy need product and compliance review.
- Competing public Invity Turbo sources cite 0.49% plus card fee and 1.49% per transaction. Show both sources dated and confirm the applicable payment method in-app before making a comparison.
- Old claims that Turbo solves fee competitiveness, custody explains KYC drop-off, or regular buying guarantees better results are withdrawn. Keep in historical archive clearly marked.
- The public visualbook could not be accessed during brand research. Palette and style are inferred from the public site and editorial artwork. Fonts and generated logo are not verified licensed brand assets.
- Actual first-purchase video and app screens require approved material. The plan uses storyboards, stills and GIF, with no video production.
- Market order, specific campaign eligibility, budgets, actual performance and available internal data are open until confirmed with Invity.
- Visual approvals below concern the proposal, not Invity’s sign-off.
