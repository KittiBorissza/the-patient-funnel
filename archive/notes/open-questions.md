# Open questions and assumptions (v2.0.0)

Things the main page treats as hypotheses, and what to ask the Invity team. Product facts were read on invity.io on 9 Nov 2026.

## Product facts used on the main page (from invity.io)
- Fees: "transparent per-transaction fees displayed before you confirm any purchase" (FAQ). No fee percentage is quoted on the page.
- Custody: "held by a regulated institutional custodian, segregated from Invity's operating funds"; BitGo named as custodian; withdraw to own wallet "at any time"; Auto Send to any address once the balance reaches a set amount (FAQ, home page).
- Onboarding: "Download, verify ID, fund" (home page).
- Auto Buy: regular automatic purchases at a chosen amount and schedule; single purchases also supported (personal accounts page).
- Academy exists in the app (About us timeline, 2024).

## To check with Invity
1. Can a real first purchase be filmed in the app, and which screens may be shown?
2. Exact wording for fees on the confirmation screen, and whether there is any Invity fee on withdrawals beyond the Bitcoin network fee.
3. How Invity wants custody and BitGo described in social content.
4. Which audience and market the team would prioritise; is Czechia the right start?
5. Whether Auto Buy or a single first purchase should be the main call to action, and for whom.
6. What tracking exists from social to install, sign-up, verification and first purchase; how attribution is handled.
7. Where real customer questions can be collected (support tool, community, reviews) and how to use them with consent.
8. Compliance review process and turnaround for social content; risk-warning wording.
9. Who can provide native Czech/German review.
10. Why people drop out during sign-up or verification. Not assumed to be custody concerns; this needs data or interviews.

## Withdrawn from v1.1.0
- "Small amounts over time beat one dramatic bet."
- Comparing an eBook opt-in with an app download as the same conversion.
- Custody uncertainty as the presumed reason for KYC drop-off.
- Turbo Buy as a response to fee competitiveness.
- Fixed thresholds such as a set number of creators.
